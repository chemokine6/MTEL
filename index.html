Multiscale Tissue Engineering Laboratory

We aim to implement bio-inspired principles through AI and apply them to tissue engineering and regenerative medicine.

Our Research Contact Us

Research

Our lab develops AI-driven, bio-inspired platforms that bridge nanomaterials, peptides, and living systems for tissue regeneration and therapeutic applications.

AI-based de novo design

We design peptides and proteins from scratch using structure prediction and generative models. By learning from natural sequences and structures, we create novel biomolecules with tailored functions for tissue engineering and regenerative medicine.

Cascade nanozyme

We engineer multi-enzyme-mimetic nanoparticles that perform cascaded reactions, such as ROS conversion and oxygen generation. These cascade nanozymes modulate the tissue microenvironment, alleviate oxidative stress, and support cell survival and function in damaged tissues.

Biomimetic functional materials

We develop self-assembling peptides, peptide–polymer hybrids, and nano–biohybrid materials that mimic extracellular matrix and cellular interfaces. These biomimetic materials provide cell-instructive cues for adhesion, differentiation, and tissue formation.

Tissue engineering applications

We integrate AI-designed biomolecules, cascade nanozymes, and biomimetic materials into 3D constructs and in vivo models. Our goal is to restore function in damaged tissues and organs through multiscale strategies spanning molecules, cells, and living systems.
