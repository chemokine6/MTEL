Research

Our lab focuses on the design and engineering of biomaterials and nano/biohybrid systems for tissue regeneration and therapeutic applications.

Nanozyme & Catalytic Nanomaterials

Design of enzyme-mimetic nanoparticles (nanozymes) for ROS management, oxygen generation, and microenvironment modulation in diseased tissues.

Peptide-based Biomaterials

Self-assembling peptides and peptide–polymer hybrids for 3D scaffolds, drug delivery, and cell-instructive microenvironments.

Tissue Engineering & Regenerative Medicine

Multiscale strategies combining biomaterials, cells, and biophysical cues to restore function in damaged tissues and organs.

AI-guided Protein/Peptide Design

Computational design of peptides and proteins with tailored functions using structure prediction and machine learning tools.
